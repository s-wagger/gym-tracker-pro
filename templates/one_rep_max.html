Standard percentage brackets (50%, 75%, 85%, 90% 1RM) for structured hypertrophy & strength programming.

📚 Periodization Programming Standards

Hypertrophy Block (70% - 80% 1RM)

Focus on high volume (8-12 reps per set), metabolic stress, and time under tension to stimulate muscle protein synthesis and cross-sectional area growth.

Strength Block (80% - 90% 1RM)

Focus on myofibrillar tension and motor unit synchronization (3-6 reps per set). Heavy compound loads with 3-5 minute recovery intervals.

Peaking & Testing (90% - 100% 1RM)

Taper volume by 40-50% while sustaining high neural intensity (1-3 reps). Maximizes neurological readiness for testing all-time PRs.
